Venue

The venue is IDA Conference (Ingeniørernes Hus) in the heart of Copenhagen city, overlooking the harbour. It is close to a number of hotels, public transport and most of the major sights in Copenhagen.

Practical Information

We refer to the Wikitravel pages for Denmark and Copenhagen for common information. Copenhagen is extremely safe and it is generally safe to walk around outside at all hours.
