Welcome to the website of CPM 2027.

The 38th Annual Symposium on Combinatorial Pattern Matching will bring together researchers working in algorithms, data structures, string algorithms, and related areas.

Conference

CPM 2027 will provide an opportunity for researchers from around the world to present their latest results and discuss new developments in combinatorial pattern matching.
